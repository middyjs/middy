## How to read this document

Status legend:

- :white_check_mark: **Implemented** - control is in place and file-verifiable.
- :shield: **Attested** - control is enforced at the GitHub organisation or workstation level and cannot be expressed in repository files; verified out of band by the maintainers.
- :no_entry_sign: **N/A** - control does not apply to this project; the Evidence column says why.
- :large_orange_diamond: **Partial** - mechanism present, with a known gap stated in the Evidence column.
- :x: **Not implemented** - control applies and is not in place.
- :warning: **Risk accepted** - control applies but is deliberately not implemented; the decision and compensating controls are in the Evidence column and re-reviewed at each annual re-attestation.

Control IDs, names, and levels come from the published SPVS 2.0 requirements CSV in [OWASP/www-project-spvs](https://github.com/OWASP/www-project-spvs) (`2.0/OWASP_SPVS_2.0_-en_Requirements.csv`). Each control is assigned to one level, and a higher level includes every control below it.

A level is met only when every control at that level and below is Implemented, Attested, or N/A. Partial, Not implemented, and Risk accepted controls all block the level they belong to: a risk acceptance records a deliberate decision, but it does not count as meeting the control.

## AI controls scope

SPVS 2.0 merges the former AI pipeline addendum into the main catalogue. middy's position:

- No workflow in `.github/workflows/` calls a model, runs an AI agent, or connects to an MCP or tool server. Jobs in egress block mode would refuse an unlisted model endpoint.
- The published packages contain no model, prompt, or AI framework.
- Contributors may use AI coding agents on their own workstations under the policy in [WORKSTATION.md](WORKSTATION.md) (AI coding assistants). Those agents act under the contributor's own account and hold no identity, credential, or permission of their own. Their output passes the same server-side gates as any other change.

Controls about agents running in the pipeline, agent identities and credentials, prompt and tool handling, model sourcing, system prompts, AIBOMs, and agent logging and forensics are therefore N/A. Controls about developer-side AI use are assessed like any other control. Adding AI to the pipeline requires a governance review and an update to this document first.
